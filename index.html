About the Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering was started in 2001 with an intake of 60 students. Today it is one of the largest departments of the college, offering undergraduate and postgraduate programmes.

Our mission is to produce engineers who can solve real-world problems using computing. The department has a strong record in university results and campus placements.

Academic Programmes

The department offers B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (4 years) and M.Tech in Data Science (2 years).

Core Subjects Offered

  1. Data Structures and Algorithms
  2. Database Management Systems
  3. Operating Systems
  4. Computer Networks
  5. Web Programming
  6. Machine Learning
